✨ About Me ✨

Hi and welcome to my corner of the World Wide Web!!! I'm Ivan, a Senior Frontend Developer from Warsaw, currently at Nearmap. I've been making websites for over five years and I still enjoy it, mostly because the job is never really done. I care about the details people only notice when they're missing: fast pages, readable text, and buttons that still work when you use a keyboard.

My hobbies include: centering divs, arguing about CSS-in-JS, and writing chiptunes for my own homepage (press ▶ in the sidebar). 😎
